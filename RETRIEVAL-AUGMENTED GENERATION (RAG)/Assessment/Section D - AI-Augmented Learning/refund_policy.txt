Food Delivery Refund Policy

Customers are eligible for a full refund if the order is cancelled by the restaurant.

If the order is delivered more than 60 minutes late, customers may receive a partial refund.

Refund requests must be submitted within 24 hours of delivery.

No refund will be issued if the incorrect delivery address was provided by the customer.

Damaged or missing items can be reported within 24 hours for compensation.

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after approval.
